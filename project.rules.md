# Portfolio Project Requirements & Guidelines

## Overview
A minimalist, clean developer portfolio website built with Next.js 14 to showcase professional experience, skills, and insights. It is designed to be simple, easy to read, and aesthetically gentle.

## Core Sections

### 1. Introduction Section
- Professional summary and personal brand statement.
- Key skills and technology expertise.
- Professional photo and contact information.
- Direct links to social media and professional networks.
- Personal mission statement and career objectives.

### 2. Skills Matrix Section
- Clean, intuitive skills proficiency visualization.
- Technical skills with proficiency levels.
- Soft skills and tools rating.
- Simple learning progress indicators.

### 3. Career Path Section
- Professional experience timeline with simple visual markers.
- Key achievements and quantifiable results.
- Notable projects with business impact metrics.
- Educational background and certifications.

### 4. Leadership & Collaboration
- Team collaboration examples and outcomes.
- Project leadership experiences.
- Mentorship and knowledge sharing initiatives.

### 5. Professional Growth
- Career development roadmap.
- Learning goals and milestones.
- Future technology interests.

### 6. Blog Section
- Technical articles, tutorials, and development stories.
- Simple categorizations and tags for easy navigation.
- Regular updates on learning journey.

### 7. Contact Section
- Social media profiles (LinkedIn, GitHub) and email contact.
- Calendly scheduling or contact form with simple purpose selection.
- Location/timezone and response time expectations.
- Direct Resume/CV download option.

## Technical & Design Requirements
- **Framework**: Next.js 14 (App Router) and TypeScript.
- **Styling**: Responsive, simple, and clean layout with Tailwind CSS.
- **Aesthetic**: Prioritize a gentle, soft pastel/light color scheme. Avoid dark neon colors or highly complex design elements.
- **Language**: Direct native text implementation (no translation files or i18n libraries required).
- **Transitions**: Subtle and natural micro-animations (hover transitions, opacity fades) rather than complex animation engines.
- **SEO & Accessibility**: Fully optimized SEO tags (metadata configurations) and accessible HTML elements (correct header hierarchy and ARIA labels).
